<h1 class="page-title">Finding the Seminar</h1>

<p class="page-lead">The seminar meets in the <strong>Qualcomm Institute Auditorium</strong>, on the ground
floor of <strong>Atkinson Hall</strong> — the building by the bear sculpture.
Mondays, 11:00–11:50 am.</p>

<div class="prose" markdown="1">

Atkinson Hall sits on the north side of the engineering quad, a few minutes' walk
from Geisel Library. If you already know the **Computer Science & Engineering
building (EBU3B)**, Atkinson Hall is right next door to the northwest — the two
are officially EBU3a and EBU3b.

The **bear sculpture** is the easiest landmark — the auditorium is on the ground
floor, a few steps from it. The building's street address is 3195 Voigt Dr.

**No food or drink other than water** is allowed in the auditorium.

</div>
