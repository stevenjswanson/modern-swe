<h1 class="page-title">Finding the Seminar</h1>

<p class="page-lead">The seminar meets in the <strong>Qualcomm Institute Auditorium</strong>, on the ground
floor of <strong>Atkinson Hall</strong> at UC San Diego — the building next to the bear sculpture.</p>

<div class="prose" markdown="1">

Atkinson Hall is on the north side of the engineering quad, a few minutes' walk
from Geisel Library. If you know the **Computer Science & Engineering building
(EBU3B)**, Atkinson Hall is the building immediately northwest of it — the two
are adjacent, and are officially EBU3a and EBU3b.

Look for the **bear sculpture** outside; the auditorium entrance is on the ground floor.

**No food or drink other than water** is allowed in the auditorium.

</div>

### Parking

Two options, both shown above:

| | Walk to the auditorium |
|---|---|
| **Lot 503** — the small lot just west of Atkinson Hall | about **2 minutes** (150 m) |
| **Hopkins Parking Structure** — larger, more likely to have space | about **6 minutes** (475 m) |

Park in spaces marked with a **white V** (visitor). Pay through the **ParkMobile**
app using **zone 4752**, or at the pay kiosk by the **7th-floor elevators** in the
Hopkins structure.

Rates at last check: first 30 minutes free, then $4.20/hour, $33.60 daily maximum.
After 5 PM and on weekends, $2.10/hour with an $8.40 maximum. *Rates and zones
change — please confirm at the kiosk when you arrive.*

### Arriving by Uber or Lyft

Set your destination to **Atkinson Hall, UC San Diego**. Campus building names
confuse some drivers, so this link drops a pin on the exact spot:

**[Navigate to Atkinson Hall &rarr;](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=32.8824734%2C-117.2347302)**

You can also paste the coordinates `32.8824734, -117.2347302` directly into Uber
or Lyft. The campus mailing address, 9500 Gilman Drive, will *not* get you to the
right building.

### Where we'll meet

Depending on what we arranged by email, we will meet either **at the auditorium**
in Atkinson Hall, or **at the chair's office** in the Computer Science &
Engineering building.

To find the chair's office — **room 266**:

1. Go in the **front entrance** of the Computer Science & Engineering building (EBU3B, the blue building on the map).
2. Go **up the stairs**.
3. It is on your **left**, toward the big sign reading *Computer Science and Engineering*.

If you get lost, call or email and we will come find you.
